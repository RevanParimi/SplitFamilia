SplitFamilia · v2

Design notes

Friendlier and simpler. White background, one rounded sans for headings, one brand colour plus a warm accent, and a colour for each person. Each screen has one obvious main action, and everything else is one tap away. See the v2 prototype and the Play Store assets.

Principles

  • One main action per screen. Welcome: Start / Join. Group home: Add expense. Sheets: one filled button in the footer.
  • Home shows only what you check: group name, sync dot, people avatars, balances, expenses. Invite link, currency, switch group and people management live in the group menu (tap the name or ⋯).
  • Tap the thing itself. Tap a balance to record a payment; tap an expense to edit it; tap the avatars to manage people.
  • Guide new groups: a single card walks through 1 Add people → 2 First expense → 3 Invite your group, with one button each. It replaces the balances until there are 2+ people and an expense.
  • Quiet sync. A small white dot when live or connecting (pulsing). Words only for "offline: …", "N changes waiting to sync" and "not syncing".

Two families: Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter. Amounts are right-aligned, tabular-nums, never wrap, and grouped en-IN. Names and descriptions use overflow-wrap:anywhere.

Spacing and corners

Spacing on a 4px base: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32. Page gutter 20 + safe-area. Corners: 12 small buttons · 14 inputs · 16–18 main buttons · 20–22 cards · 24 sheet top and dialogs · pill for avatars and status.

Components

Buttons
Add expenseJoin with a linkChangeDelete
Filled brand for the one main action, outline brand for the alternative, soft brand for inline "Change", red outline for Delete (filled red only inside a confirmation). Minimum 44px, main actions 56px. Focus: 3px brand outline.
Header
Brand-coloured band: the group name (tap it to open the menu) with a chevron, sync dot, ⋯ button, then the avatar row with a dashed + and "4 people". White text passes on all three brand colours.
Rows
Balance: payer avatar, "Ben owes Asha", red amount, chevron. The whole row records a payment. Expense: payer avatar, description, "29 Sep · paid by Asha · split among 3", amount. Payment: avatar with a green arrow badge, green "Ben paid Asha", "· payment". Unsynced: grey "waiting to sync" tag, faint amount. Menu row: 44px icon circle, label and one-line hint, 64px tall.
Sheets
One bottom-sheet shell (24px top corners, 42% scrim, handle, title, round close) used for Group menu, People, Start a group, Join, Add/Edit expense and Record payment. The footer holds the one main button above the safe area. At 700px and wider, sheets become centred 560px panels. In Add expense, only two fields take typing: Paid by starts on the latest payer, and the split is one line with "Change" that opens the checkboxes. An error on the split opens them automatically.
Guide card
Accent-soft card: "Step N of 3" with a three-dash progress bar, a title, one sentence and one dark button. Step 3 also offers "Not now".
Dialogs and messages
Confirmations are centred alertdialogs that name the item: "Delete 'Dinner' (₹1,200.00)?", "Remove Chitra from the group?". The message bar is red-soft with role="alert" and a 44px dismiss, floating above the Add expense button. Field errors sit under their field with aria-invalid and aria-describedby.

Where everything moved

  • Copy invite link, Currency, Switch group → group menu (name or ⋯). Copy invite link is also step 3 of the guide.
  • Add or remove people → People sheet (tap the avatars, or +). The blocked-removal message shows inline under that person.
  • Record payment → tap a balance. Edit or delete → tap an expense.
  • Remove from this device → "Edit" next to Your groups.
  • Join paste field → the "Join with a link" sheet.
