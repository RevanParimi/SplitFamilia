# SF-002 — Guide to setting up the Google Play developer account

Priority: **P0** · Task: **T-02** (story 1 of 5) · Estimate: **1 point**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not in this card. Dependencies: none.

Brief: [stage 2](../brief/2026-09-28-google-play-release-brief.md).

## Intended result

The owner has one document with every step to create and verify the Google Play developer
account and create the app entry. It comes early because the slow parts, identity verification
and (for new personal accounts) a closed test that runs for weeks, can then start while the code
work continues.

## Starting points

- No code. Use the official Play Console Help and Android Developers pages (WebSearch and
  WebFetch), and record the date each fact was checked.
- Decisions this guide feeds: D-1 (package name), D-2 (app name), D-7 (personal or organization
  account). See `open_decisions` in STATE.json.

## Acceptance criteria

- `docs/google-play/PLAY_CONSOLE_SETUP.md` covers:
  - personal vs organization accounts: what each needs (for example a D-U-N-S number for
    organizations) and which testing rules apply to each;
  - developer identity verification;
  - the contact details Google asks for, and which of them the store shows publicly;
  - the registration fee;
  - choosing the package name: its format, that it is unique, and that it can never change
    after the first upload;
  - creating the app in Play Console;
  - Play App Signing;
  - the upload key and the app signing key, and what to do if the upload key is lost.
- Every step the owner must do is labelled **MANUAL ACTION REQUIRED**.
- Anything that depends on the owner's own account is labelled **VERIFY IN PLAY CONSOLE**.
  Nothing account-specific is made up: no names, IDs or email addresses.
- Facts that change over time link the official page and give the date checked (IST). These
  include the fee, the tester count, the number of testing days and the documents needed for
  verification.
- A "Decisions" section records:
  - D-1: `com.splitfamilia.app`, a neutral ID that keeps the owner's name out of it;
  - D-2: SplitFamilia;
  - D-7: **personal** account (chosen 2026-09-28). Its cost is the closed test for new personal
    accounts: 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days (as of 2026-09-28; VERIFY IN PLAY
    CONSOLE).
- **What Google shows publicly** for a personal account: the developer display name (can be
  "SplitFamilia"), plus the **legal name, country and developer email**. The owner prefers a
  low profile, so recommend a separate brand email for the developer contact, and cite
  [Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312).
- A "Start recruiting testers now" note, with the steps to collect testers' Google account
  emails.
- The doc says plainly that nothing in this repository creates or changes the Play account.

## Required tests

- Every manual step carries its label, which a grep can check.
- Every external link opens (WebFetch) and supports the claim it is cited for.
- No personal data about the owner appears in the doc.
