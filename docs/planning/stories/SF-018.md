# SF-018 — Publish the privacy policy page and link it inside the app

Priority: **P0** · Task: **T-06** (story 2 of 5) · Estimate: **1 point**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not in this card. Dependencies: SF-011, SF-017.

Brief: [stage 5 and stage 8 ("privacy policy publicly
accessible")](../brief/2026-09-28-google-play-release-brief.md).

## Intended result

The privacy policy is a real page on the production site, for example
`https://<host>/privacy.html`, and both the app's footer and its join screen link to it. Play
requires a privacy policy link both in the store listing and inside the app.

## Starting points

- `docs/google-play/PRIVACY_POLICY.md` (SF-017).
- `index.html`: the footer (lines 457–459) and the join card (lines 374–384).
- `service-worker.js` `SHELL`.

## Acceptance criteria

- `privacy.html` matches the approved policy text, in the app's styling, and reads well at phone
  width.
- It is linked from the footer and the join screen.
- It is cached in the service-worker shell, so it opens offline. Bump the cache name.
- **Owner gate:** until the owner approves the policy text, the page keeps a visible "Draft"
  banner and `production_verification` stays `pending_deployment`. Add a `pending_check`: owner
  approval, then deploy, then the URL returns 200 over HTTPS.
- The final URL is recorded in `STORE_LISTING.md` and `PLAY_CONSOLE_DECLARATIONS.md`, replacing
  `<PRIVACY_POLICY_URL>`.

## Required tests

- Manual: both links open the page, and the page opens offline after a visit.

## Follow-ups from T-04 (2026-10-01)

- Since T-04 the page has no footer. Good places for the privacy link: the welcome screen's
  small print under "Join with a link" (`.fine-print`) and the group menu's note
  (`.menu-note`). Add `privacy.html` to the worker's `SHELL` only if it should open offline.

## Follow-ups from the T-04 review (2026-10-01)

See [the T-04 review](../evidence/T-04-review.md). This story is the next to change
`index.html`, so fix these here:

- **F-14 (low): the message bar is hidden while a sheet is open.** `#notice` sits outside the
  `<dialog>` sheets, so a message raised while a sheet or the confirmation is open (a refused
  change, "sync stopped", "Something went wrong") is drawn under it, and is inert to screen
  readers, until the sheet closes. Probe P4 shows it: an offline edit beaten by another phone's
  delete is refused while "Add expense" is open, and the bar can't be seen
  ([screenshot](../evidence/T-04-review-notice-under-sheet.png)).
  - Fix: show the bar in the top layer, for example `popover="manual"` with `showPopover()`
    whenever it's shown and again after a sheet opens, or move it into the open dialog. Keep
    `role="alert"` and the texts word for word.
  - Check: re-run P4 of `evidence/T-04-review-probe.mjs`; expect `noticeOnTop: true` with the
    sheet open.
- **N-14 (note):** `openExpense` and `openPay` set `form` or `pay` before `openSheet` runs the
  previous sheet's close hook, which sets them back to null. That only matters where a panel
  can be opened over itself, that is in the plain-dialog fallback without `showModal` (iOS
  before 15.4). If convenient, run the old hook before setting the new state.

## Rollout and rollback

- Web deploy (owner's word) with a cache bump. Rolling back is a revert, but Play needs a live
  policy, so replace the page rather than remove it.
