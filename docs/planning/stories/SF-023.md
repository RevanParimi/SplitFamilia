# SF-023 — Record a settle-up

Priority: **P1** · Task: **T-04** (story 2 of 5) · Estimate: **2 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not in this card. Dependencies: SF-004, SF-007.

## Intended result

When Ben hands Asha ₹100 in cash, anyone can tap "Ben paid Asha ₹100" under Balances, and the
ledger records it so the balances drop to zero. The app only **records** that a payment happened
outside the app. It never moves money, so it stays out of "financial features" (SF-016 must say
so).

## Starting points

- `index.html` `renderBalances()` (lines 739–769) and `money.js`.

## Acceptance criteria

- A settlement is stored as an expense paid by the debtor with the creditor as the only split
  member, marked `kind: "settlement"`. Old data without `kind` keeps working.
- It shows in the ledger as "Ben paid Asha", not as a normal expense.
- The rules and their tests accept the new field.
- Update the declarations and testing docs if they exist.

## Required tests

- A owes B ₹100; record A→B ₹100 → both balances are 0.
- A partial settlement of ₹40 → A owes B ₹60.
