# SF-024 — Delete a whole group from the app (stretch)

Priority: **P2** · Task: **T-07** (story 2 of 5) · Estimate: **2 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not in this card. Dependencies: SF-007. **Stretch:**
do it only if the owner promotes it.

## Intended result

A family whose trip is over can erase the whole group (its people, its expenses and its
settings) from inside the app, after typing the group's name to confirm. Users then control
deletion of their own data without emailing the owner.

**Risk for the owner to weigh:** with no accounts, anyone who has the link can delete the group
for everyone.

## Starting points

- `index.html` `startApp()` and the group bar (lines 397–403).
- `firestore.rules` (SF-007).

## Acceptance criteria

- A "Delete this group" action, with a typed-name confirmation, deletes every person and every
  expense and then the group document, in batches.
- Afterwards the app returns to the join screen and clears the stored group.
- The rules allow deleting the group document, with emulator tests.
- Update `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` and the Data safety answers ("users can delete their data").

## Required tests

- Emulator: the delete leaves no documents under `groups/{gid}`.
- Manual: a second phone sees the group become empty.

## Changed by the move to Railway (2026-09-30)

The owner moved the database to Railway too, with no dependency on Firebase (T-08, T-09).

- Deleting a group becomes one API call. The server removes the group, its people and its
  expenses in one transaction. No Firestore rules are involved.
- From T-08's implementation: deleting a person or an expense only marks the row
  (`deleted_at`). Deleting the group must really remove every row, marked ones included:
  `DELETE FROM groups` cascades to `people`, `expenses` and `expense_split`. After that, a late
  add or delete from a phone gets 404. A late currency change (`PUT /api/group`) would create an
  empty group under the same code, so the delete should also keep the code as deleted.
- The T-09 review confirmed this in the browser as **F-12**: a currency change on the link of
  an unknown code creates the group. Its fix is on SF-022's card; if SF-022 hasn't fixed it by
  then, this story must.

## Rollout and rollback

- Web and rules deploys (the owner's word). Deleted data cannot be recovered; say so in the UI.
