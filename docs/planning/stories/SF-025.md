# SF-025 — Serve the fonts from our own site (stretch)

Priority: **P2** · Task: **T-07** (story 3 of 5) · Estimate: **1 point**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not in this card. Dependencies: SF-008. **Stretch:**
do it only if the owner promotes it.

## Intended result

The app stops calling Google Fonts on every launch. It looks the same, it looks right offline,
and one third party drops out of the privacy policy and the Data safety answers.

## Starting points

- `index.html` lines 15–17 (Google Fonts: Source Serif 4, Inter and IBM Plex Mono). All three
  are under the SIL Open Font License, which allows self-hosting. Include the licence file.

## Acceptance criteria

- The `woff2` files for the weights in use are served from the site, with `@font-face` rules
  and the licence text in the repo.
- There are no requests to `fonts.googleapis.com` or `fonts.gstatic.com` (check the DevTools
  Network tab).
- The fonts are cached in the service-worker shell. Bump the cache name.
- Update the privacy policy and declarations if they exist.

## Required tests

- Manual: the page looks the same (a screenshot before and after), and the Network tab shows no
  Google Fonts requests.

## Rollout and rollback

- Web deploy with a cache bump. Rolling back is a revert.
