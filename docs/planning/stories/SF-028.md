# SF-028 — Polished, good-looking mobile UI

Priority: **P1** · Task: **T-04** (story 4 of 5) · Estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not in this card. Dependencies: SF-010, SF-022,
SF-023, SF-029.

Owner direction (D-4, 2026-09-28): ship with good features and a good-looking UI, keep improving
after release, and aim for solid rather than high-end.

## Intended result

On a phone, SplitFamilia looks like a finished app worth screenshotting for the store. Taps are
easy, deleting asks first, and the balances are the first thing you see. Every existing feature
still works, and the calm paper-and-ink style stays; this is a polish, not a redesign.

## Starting points

- `index.html`: the `<style>` block (lines 18–369) and the layout (lines 387–460).
- The existing palette tokens (`--paper`, `--ink`, `--green`, `--rust`).
- Screens: join, people, add expense, ledger, balances.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every tap target is at least 44×44 CSS px. Today the × buttons on chips and ledger rows are
  about 15 px.
- Deleting an expense or removing a person asks for confirmation, naming the item, e.g. "Delete
  'Dinner' (₹1,200.00)?"
- The balances summary sits near the top (the "who owes whom" card). The add-expense form can be
  collapsed or reached with a clear button.
- Safe areas are respected (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`), with no horizontal scroll at 360 px wide.
  Layouts are checked at 360, 390 and 412 px and on a tablet width.
- Ledger dates show the year when it isn't the current year.
- Focus states are visible, labels are tied to their inputs, and the colour contrast meets WCAG
  AA for text.
- No new third-party libraries. Bump the service-worker cache name.
- Before and after screenshots at 390 px wide are recorded in the receipt. SF-015 reuses the
  after shots.

## Required tests

- A manual browser pass over every screen, at each width above.
- `npm test` is still green, and the money output is unchanged.

## Follow-up from the T-02 review (2026-09-29)

- **N-1 (note):** a link pasted under "Join" with a trailing `.`, `,` or `)` from a sentence,
  such as `…?g=goa-trip-7k2m9xqpwd.`, is refused as invalid. Consider trimming trailing
  punctuation in `parseInvite()` (with a test), so a pasted message still works. See
  [the T-02 review](../evidence/T-02-review.md).

## Follow-ups from T-03 (2026-09-29)

- T-03 added a start-up panel (`#boot`: "Loading…", or "Can't reach SplitFamilia. Check your
  connection." with Retry) and one message bar (`#notice`) for save, sync and unexpected errors.
  Restyle them with the rest; keep the texts plain and keep `role="alert"`.
- Form checks ("Add a description.", amount errors) still use `alert()`. Moving them into the
  page is part of the polish.
- A ledger row the server hasn't confirmed shows "waiting to sync" (`.ledger-sync`).
