# SF-028 — Polished, good-looking mobile UI

Priority: **P1** · Task: **T-04** (story 4 of 5) · Estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not in this card. Dependencies: SF-010, SF-022,
SF-023, SF-029.

Owner direction (D-4, 2026-09-28): ship with good features and a good-looking UI, keep improving
after release, and aim for solid rather than high-end.

## Intended result

On a phone, SplitFamilia looks like a finished app worth screenshotting for the store. Taps are
easy, deleting asks first, and the balances are the first thing you see. Every existing feature
still works, and the calm paper-and-ink style stays; this is a polish, not a redesign.

## Starting points

- `index.html`: the `<style>` block (lines 18–369) and the layout (lines 387–460).
- The existing palette tokens (`--paper`, `--ink`, `--green`, `--rust`).
- Screens: join, people, add expense, ledger, balances.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every tap target is at least 44×44 CSS px. Today the × buttons on chips and ledger rows are
  about 15 px.
- Deleting an expense or removing a person asks for confirmation, naming the item, e.g. "Delete
  'Dinner' (₹1,200.00)?"
- The balances summary sits near the top (the "who owes whom" card). The add-expense form can be
  collapsed or reached with a clear button.
- Safe areas are respected (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`), with no horizontal scroll at 360 px wide.
  Layouts are checked at 360, 390 and 412 px and on a tablet width.
- Ledger dates show the year when it isn't the current year.
- Focus states are visible, labels are tied to their inputs, and the colour contrast meets WCAG
  AA for text.
- No new third-party libraries. Bump the service-worker cache name.
- Before and after screenshots at 390 px wide are recorded in the receipt. SF-015 reuses the
  after shots.

## Required tests

- A manual browser pass over every screen, at each width above.
- `npm test` is still green, and the money output is unchanged.

## Design reference from Claude Design (owner, 2026-09-29)

- The owner is making the look in Claude Design, from
  [docs/design/CLAUDE_DESIGN_PROMPT.md](../../design/CLAUDE_DESIGN_PROMPT.md). Its export (HTML,
  notes, screenshots) goes in `docs/design/`. If it is there when T-04 starts, SF-028 follows it
  as the reference: port the look into `index.html`, keep every id, handler and behaviour, and
  never paste the export over `index.html` (it has no Firebase, offline or sync wiring).
- **Owner direction, 2026-09-29, after the first prototype:** the paper-and-serif look felt too
  much like a generic "Claude" style. The owner wants it a little more colourful, with a matching
  theme (not a big change), and much simpler for a first-time user: one main action per screen,
  secondary actions in one group menu, and a guided first run. This replaces "the calm
  paper-and-ink style stays" above. Follow-up prompt 2 in the design prompt file has the details;
  the owner picks one of three palettes.
- **The export is in the repo (2026-09-30):**
  - [DESIGN_NOTES.md](../../design/DESIGN_NOTES.md) is the spec: tokens (indigo `#4F46E5` brand,
    saffron `#F4A51C` accent, six person colours), type (Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter; Source
    Serif 4 and IBM Plex Mono are dropped), spacing, radius, every component, and "where
    everything moved". The palette is **indigo-saffron**.
  - `docs/design/prototype/SplitFamilia-Prototype-v2.html` is the clickable prototype, with every
    screen and state (its `screen` switcher). It's a Claude Design bundle that runs on React 18,
    packed inside the file. Use it to look at, never as code: the app stays plain HTML, CSS and
    JavaScript with no libraries.
  - Its sample invite links use `splitfamilia.app`, which isn't the app's address. The real host
    is `https://splitfamilia.up.railway.app` (T-05's note).
  - `docs/design/playstore-mockups/` holds six 1080 × 1920 mock-up screens and a 1024 × 500
    feature graphic (see SF-015).
- The design changes behaviour only in the interface: "Paid by" starts on the most recent
  expense's payer; the split is "everyone" until changed; a three-step guide shows until a group
  has 2+ people and an expense; secondary actions live in a group menu. It adds no stored data
  beyond SF-029's recent-groups list. New wording ("Nobody owes anybody.", "Tap one to record a
  payment") is fine; the status and error texts stay word for word.
- Where the design differs from these acceptance criteria, the criteria win unless the owner
  says otherwise; record each difference in the receipt. If the export isn't there yet, ask
  the owner before starting SF-028, and do SF-022, SF-023 and SF-029 first.
