# SF-030 — Split an expense unequally (stretch)

Priority: **P2** · Task: **T-07** (story 1 of 5) · Estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not in this card. Dependencies: SF-004, SF-007.
**Stretch:** do it only if the owner promotes it.

## Intended result

Asha paid ₹1,000 for a hotel room that Ben used for 2 nights and Chitra for 1. The app can split
it 2:1 by shares, or by exact amounts, instead of only equally.

## Starting points

- `money.js` (SF-001, SF-004) and the add-expense form (`index.html` lines 419–444).
- `firestore.rules` (SF-007): the expense shape.

## Acceptance criteria

- The split mode can be equal (the default and today's behaviour), exact amounts, or shares.
- Exact amounts must add up to the total before saving.
- Shares are split in paise by largest remainder: each member gets the floor of their exact
  share, and the leftover paise go to the largest fractional parts, with ties broken by person
  ID. For equal splits this gives the same result as SF-004.
- Stored as an optional `weights` or `amounts` field. Old expenses without it still split
  equally.
- The rules and their emulator tests accept the new field.

## Required tests

- ₹1,000 (100000 paise) by shares 2:1 → exact 66666.67 and 33333.33 → floors 66666 and 33333
  plus 1 leftover to the larger remainder → 66667 + 33333.
- Exact amounts that don't add up are rejected.
- Old data gives the same balances as before.
